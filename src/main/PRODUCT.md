# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mentora serves students and mentors who need to discover, organize, and complete learning activities without first learning a complex administrative system. Students need clear next steps and progress context. Mentors need efficient teaching and classroom workflows that remain approachable.

## Product Purpose

Mentora supports learning, mentoring, classroom participation, and progress tracking. Success means users can understand the current context, find the next relevant action, and complete core tasks with low cognitive load across desktop and mobile.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, clear, and academic. The interface should feel modern and clean while keeping learning content, instructional context, and task completion ahead of decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid ERP-like and traditional admin-dashboard density: crowded data tables, simultaneous information overload, deep menus, and competing actions. Avoid excessive accent colors and decorative effects. Do not remove so much guidance that first-time users must guess how a workflow operates.

Useful references are Coursera, Notion, Canvas LMS, and Google Classroom, especially their understandable navigation, restrained visual systems, and focus on content and task completion.

## Design Principles

1. Put learning content and the user's next task first.
2. Use clear hierarchy and progressive disclosure to reduce cognitive load.
3. Keep navigation predictable and provide contextual guidance when needed.
4. Prefer a restrained, consistent visual vocabulary over competing accents.
5. Use motion and effects only when they communicate state or support an interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA on desktop and mobile. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, accessible names and form feedback, sufficient contrast, text resizing, reduced motion, and touch targets appropriate for mobile use.
